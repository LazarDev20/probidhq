Data Capture

ProBid captures three types of actual job data:

GPS Timer — One-tap start/stop timer with location verification. Confirms you're within 500m of the job site before logging hours. Works on iOS and Android.

Receipt Scan — Photo of any receipt. AI reads every line item and lets you split across multiple active jobs in seconds.

Voice Notes — Speak a job note. AI parses hours, materials, and scope changes automatically.

All data stays in your browser. Never sent to any server except AI processing.
